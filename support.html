Frequently asked questions

What is Mizanuk?

Mizanuk is a daily Islamic worship companion. It brings together prayer times and Qibla direction, Qur'an reading in both Hafs and Warsh riwayat, a dhikr counter, and gentle tracking of your daily deeds — all in one place, without turning worship into a game.

Is Mizanuk free?

The core app — prayer times, Qibla, Qur'an, and dhikr — is free for everyone. An optional premium tier covers deeper reports and level progress; it does not gate access to worship features.

Which Qur'an script does Mizanuk use?

Mizanuk uses the Uthmanic script and text provided by the King Fahd Glorious Qur'an Printing Complex (KFGQPC), used unmodified under its free-use license, in both Hafs and Warsh riwayat.

Do you sell or share my data?

No. We never sell your data or share it with advertisers, under any circumstance. See our Privacy Policy for the full detail on what we collect and why.

How do I delete my account and data?

Open the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. Your worship data and personal information are deleted within 90 days. You can also request deletion by emailing us.

I found a bug or have a feature request — where do I send it?

Email contact@mizanuk.com with as much detail as you can (what happened, your device, and steps to reproduce if it's a bug). We read every message.

What languages does Mizanuk support?

Arabic, English, and French, with full right-to-left layout in Arabic.
