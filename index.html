What Mizanuk does

Everything you need to stay consistent

Five simple tools, built around one purpose: helping you show up for your worship, honestly and without judgment.

Prayer times & Qibla

Accurate prayer times for your location, with a true-north Qibla compass that points straight to the Kaaba.

Qur'an — Hafs & Warsh

Read in either riwaya, with clean Uthmanic script from the King Fahd Glorious Qur'an Printing Complex.

Dhikr counter

Simple, distraction-free counting for your daily adhkar — default sets included, or add your own.

Daily deeds tracking

Log prayers, Nawafil, fasting, charity, and reading — a gentle daily record, not a scoreboard.

Reports & progress

See your consistency over time — weekly and monthly views that help you notice patterns, not chase points.

Private by design

Your worship data is yours. Never sold, never shared with advertisers — read our full privacy policy.

This app is not a measure of your worth before Allah, nor is it a game of collecting points. It is simply a tool to help you remain consistent and hold yourself accountable.
